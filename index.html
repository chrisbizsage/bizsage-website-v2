The problem

Your best people are doing robot work.

Walk through any finance, operations or customer service team and you’ll see it. Capable people spend their days copying data between systems, capturing invoices, chasing payments and paperwork, answering the same questions and building the same reports.

Meanwhile salaries keep rising, good people are hard to find, and growth is hard to come by. Hiring more people to do more of the same work is no longer a good answer.

What’s already possible

This isn’t a forecast. It’s happening now.

Banking · Absa, South Africa

Credit analysis cut from 5 days to 4 hours

Absa reports that AI cut the financial analysis behind credit decisions from 5 days to 4 hours, and SME account opening from 2 days to under 30 minutes.

The same people, freed from processing, spend more time on customers and decisions.

Accounts payable · global consumer goods company

92% of invoices processed with no one touching them

AI agents read invoices, coded them and chased open items. Time per invoice fell from up to 30 minutes to minutes or seconds.

People still review what the AI flags. The job moved from typing to checking.

Order processing · EVG, an electronics distributor

From 4.5 minutes per order to 16 seconds

AI now reads incoming orders and enters them into the system. The company saved about 68 working days in 1 quarter and handles busy periods without extra staff.

A mid-sized business, not a giant. This is within reach.

Customer service · Klarna

The work of 700 agents in its first month

Klarna’s AI assistant handled two-thirds of customer chats and cut resolution times from 11 minutes to under 2.

Klarna later brought more people back for quality. The winning design is AI plus people.

Questions

What leaders usually ask.

What does it cost?

A Leadership Briefing is R35,000. The Operations Assessment starts at R95,000, paid upfront. Builds start at R250,000 per department, with half on signing and the rest at agreed milestones. The partnership starts at R55,000 a month, billed monthly in advance with a six-month minimum. All prices exclude VAT, and every phase is designed to cost well below the people you’d otherwise need to hire.

Will this replace my staff?

That isn’t the goal, and we won’t promise it. Most companies use the time to grow without hiring, take on more work, or let roles evolve when people move on.

Do we need new software?

Usually not. We build on the systems you already use, such as Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, Xero, Sage, your CRM and WhatsApp.

Is our data safe?

We sign a POPIA operator agreement before touching any personal information, keep each client’s systems separate, and use AI services that don’t train on your data. People review anything important before it goes out.

Do you work outside the Western Cape?

Yes. Everything happens on Google Meet, so we work with companies anywhere in South Africa. With your permission, meetings are recorded and you receive written notes afterwards.

How much of our team’s time will it take?

A few hours from each department head during the assessment, and short weekly check-ins during each build. Our team does the heavy lifting.
